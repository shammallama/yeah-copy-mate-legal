# Privacy Policy

**Yeah Copy Mate**
Last updated: 7 November 2026

Yeah Copy Mate ("the app", "we", "us") is run by Shamma Llama Labs (ABN 56 739 677 059). This policy explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. We handle your information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

You must be 18 or over to use the app.

## 1. Our promise in plain words

- We don't sell your information.
- We don't show ads.
- Other drivers never see your email address.
- If you post anonymously, other drivers can't see who you are. Our admin team can (see section 4).
- You can delete your account and your content from inside the app at any time.

## 2. What we collect

**Information you give us**
- Your email address and password (your password is stored in scrambled form by our login provider, and we can't read it).
- Your display name.
- Your posts ("yarns"), comments, reactions and stories, including whether you chose to post anonymously or under your display name.
- Your Quick Check-In answers (how you rate your headspace).
- Messages you send to our admin team.
- Reports you file about other content.

**Information created by using the app**
- Your notification settings, and a push notification token for your device if you turn notifications on.
- A record that you accepted the safety disclaimer, and when.
- If our automatic check spots crisis-related words in something you write, we log that it happened, linked to your account (see section 5).
- If you're banned or moderated, a record of that action.

**Information we don't collect**
- Your location.
- Your contacts, photos or microphone.
- Advertising identifiers or analytics about how you use the app. If we add analytics later, we'll update this policy first.

## 3. How we use your information

- To run your account and show your posts, comments and stories.
- To send notifications you've asked for.
- To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, removing harmful content and banning people who break the rules.
- To show crisis support information when it looks like someone might need it.
- To fix problems, prevent misuse and meet our legal obligations.

## 4. Who can see what

| Information | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| Email address | Only you and our admin team |
| Posts and comments you post under your display name | Everyone using the app |
| Posts and comments you post anonymously | Everyone sees "Anonymous". Our admin team can see which account wrote it, for safety and moderation. |
| Quick Check-In answers | Only you, inside the app. People with administrator access to our database can technically read it, but we don't look at it and don't use it for anything. |
| Messages to the team | You and our admin team |
| Reports you file | Our admin team. Reported people are never told who reported them. |

Anonymous posting hides you from other drivers. It doesn't hide you from us, because we have a duty to keep the community safe.

## 5. Crisis safety

The app checks what you write for words that suggest you might be in danger. If it finds them, it shows you crisis support numbers. We record that this happened, linked to your account, so our team can make sure the safety features are working.

We aren't a crisis service and don't monitor the app around the clock. If you're in immediate danger, call 000. For 24/7 crisis support, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

In rare cases where we reasonably believe someone's life, health or safety is under serious threat, we may share relevant information with emergency services or another appropriate person or organisation. The Privacy Act permits this.

## 6. Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to run the app. They handle information on our behalf and only for that purpose:

- **Supabase** hosts our database, login and file storage.
- **Expo** and the **Apple and Google** push notification services deliver notifications to your phone.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries. We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect your information.

We may also share information if the law requires it (for example a court order), or where it is allowed under section 5.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account in the app (Profile > Delete account), we permanently delete your profile, posts, comments, reactions, stories, check-ins, notification tokens and your messages with the team.

Some records are kept after deletion with your identity removed, because we need them to keep the community safe: crisis safety logs, reports you filed, and our moderation history. These can no longer be linked to you.

## 8. Keeping your information safe

We use secure, access-controlled storage, encrypted connections, and restrict admin access to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, as required by law.

## 9. Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction:** you can see and edit most of your information in the app. For anything else, contact us and we'll help.
- **Notifications:** turn them off any time in Profile.
- **Delete your account:** Profile > Delete account.
- **Complaints:** contact us first and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
